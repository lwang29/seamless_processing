## 1. The scope change: confirmed, and one direction was unanimous

ASK 1 drew the 28 files where the two verdict scopes disagree, 14 each way. All
28 came back labelled.

| direction | n | file rule right | session rule right |
|---|---|---|---|
| file says **standing**, session said seated | 14 | **14 / 14** | 0 / 14 |
| file says **seated**, session said standing | 14 | 6 / 14 | 8 / 14 |
| **both** | 28 | **20 / 28** | 8 / 28 |

The first row is unanimous: every file the session rule was wrongly rejecting is
a standing person. The second row is a wash, and honestly so — those files were
selected precisely because the per-file measure says seated there, so it is an
adversarial draw against the rule I was proposing, and it still only breaks even.

Across every V03 label now in a scan (149 files):

| scope | sitters caught | standing lost | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| session majority | 36 / 53 | 34 / 96 | 0.658 |
| **the file itself** | **47 / 53** | **19 / 96** | **0.832** |

Settled. V03 goes by the file.

## 2. The shin add-on is pure cost on V03

Checking which clause produced the standing files FM1 was losing:

| rule | sitters caught | standing lost | accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| hip < 146° **or shin inverted** | 52 / 53 | **18 / 96** | 0.872 |
| hip < 146° **alone** | 52 / 53 | **10 / 96** | **0.926** |

The add-on flags **8 files that hip flexion does not, and not one of them is
seated.** It is the ankles-out-of-frame case you identified in Round 7: with the
feet outside the raster the fit puts them somewhere arbitrary and the shin comes
out pointing upward. Only 6 of the 18 files lost at 146° had both ankles in shot
on every frame.

So it is **off for V03**. It stays on for V00, where it fires on about one file
in 4,000 and you confirmed that file was seated.

That is the fourth V00-validated choice not to survive contact with V03, after
the raster calibration, the measure, and the verdict scope. The pattern is
consistent enough that I have written it into the notes as a standing caution
rather than four separate findings.

## 3. A number I have now revised twice

The standing cost of the 146° cut, as the label set has grown:

| labels | standing lost at 146° |
|---|---|
| 90 | 4 / 45 — 8.9% |
| 123 | 8 / 65 — 12.3% |
| **149** | **18 / 96 — 18.8%** |
| 149, shin add-on removed | **10 / 96 — 10.4%** |

It rose each time, because each earlier draw over-represented clear cases: the
first ladder was spread across the whole range, and only later rounds
concentrated on the boundary. Half of the final rise turned out to be the shin
add-on rather than the cut.

On 149 labels, with the add-on off:

| cut | sitters caught | standing lost | accuracy | V03 dyad-hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 138° | 48 / 53 | **1 / 96** | **0.960** | 149 |
| 142° | 48 / 53 | 4 / 96 | 0.946 | 148 |
| **146°** *(kept)* | **52 / 53** | 10 / 96 | 0.926 | 147 |

**146° stays.** You have twice said you would rather over-filter than leave
seated clips in, and the hours barely move — 147 against 149, about 1% of V03 —
so the recall-biased choice costs almost nothing in volume. 138° would leave 5 of
53 seated participants in the corpus to save two dyad-hours, which is the wrong
side of the trade you asked for.

The one seated file 146° still misses reads 151.9°, the top of the seated
distribution and inside the standing range (which begins at 136.1°). No cut
separates it.

## 4. Where FM1 stands, and what I would still change

```
V00   knee position < 0.43   + shin add-on   session-majority scope
V01   off                                    (fires on 0 of 1,379 square-pixel files)
V02   off                                    (181 of 182 firings were the shin add-on)
V03   hip flexion   < 146°   no add-on       per-file scope
```

Every clause has a reason and a label count behind it. **Nothing here needs more
review.** Two things remain open and neither is a posture question:

- **The measure cannot represent a posture change inside one recording.** Your
  `V03_S1821_I00000010_P3624` stands, sits, stands and sits again; its hip
  flexion runs p25 98°, median 137°, p75 161°. The median lands on the right side
  so the file is caught, but a file that sits for a third of its length would
  not be. The interquartile range is the signature and is recorded; turning it
  into a rule would need labels on *segments* rather than files, which is a
  different exercise.
- **V00 has never been re-examined under any of the four per-vendor findings.**
  Its cut, scope and add-on were all validated on 66 labels in Round 5, and they
  have not been wrong. But three of the four V03 corrections were to things that
  looked settled on V00 evidence, and V00 is 1,441 dyad-hours — the largest
  single block in the corpus.
